RAG Pipeline & AnythingLLM Integration

Using AnythingLLM as a local vector workspace, literary texts sourced from Project Gutenberg were parsed, embedded, and queried with a targeted extraction system prompt to retrieve exact flora/fauna entities with verbatim evidence.

AnythingLLM Vector Workspace Workflow

  1. Plaintext literary ingestion (.txt formats).
  2. Semantic chunking & vector indexing.
  3. Zero-hallucination prompt constraints.
  4. Verbatim evidence verification & deduplication.

System Prompt Architecture

"Act as a research assistant in digital humanities... Conduct evidence-based extraction of Indian flora and fauna from workspace documents. Categorize into 5 flora and 5 fauna groups with exact supporting quotations..."

Epistemic Bias, Abstract & Limitations

Model Pre-training Bias

Commercial LLMs trained primarily on Western datasets present notable biases when analyzing Indian ecological texts. Native species such as Amaltas (Cassia fistula) or Karaunda are often unrepresented or default-mapped to Western species (Oak, Pine, Maple) unless strict RAG vector grounding is enforced.

Corpus Sources & Citations

All analyzed texts were acquired from Project Gutenberg as plain text files (.txt). Limitations include missing OCR page numbers in plain text chunks and potential omission of highly implicit poetic metaphors.
